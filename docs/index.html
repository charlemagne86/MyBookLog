MyBookLog Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information MyBookLog ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. MyBookLog is a personal reading-log app: it lets you search for and add books to your own shelf, mark them read or unread, and rate them.

By creating an account and using MyBookLog, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used as your login username and for account-related communication (password reset, etc.)
  • First and last name — as you enter them at signup
  • Password — never stored or seen by us in plain text; authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase, which stores only a securely hashed version

Content you create

  • Searching for, locating, and adding books — you can search for a book by title or author; your search text is sent to the Google Books API to find matches, and when you add a result, its details (title, author, ISBN, cover image) are saved to your bookshelf
  • Your bookshelf — the books you've added, as above
  • Read/unread status and the date you marked a book read
  • Your personal 1–5 star rating for books on your shelf

This content is private to your account. Row-level security rules on our database mean no other user can read, modify, or delete your shelf, ratings, or profile — only you (and, operationally, our backend provider) can.

Information we do not collect

MyBookLog has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting software built in. We do not track your behavior in the app, do not show ads, and do not build an advertising profile of you. We do not sell your data to anyone, for any purpose.

Your theme color/appearance preference is stored only on your own device (never transmitted to us or anyone else).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to operate the app for you:

  • To create and secure your account, and let you log in
  • To store and display your bookshelf, read status, and ratings back to you
  • To let you search for books (your search terms are sent to the Google Books API to return results — see below)
  • To respond if you contact us for support

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond running the app.

How Your Information Is Shared

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. It's shared only with the third-party services that make the app work:

  • Supabase (our backend/database/authentication provider) — stores your account, bookshelf, and ratings on our behalf, under our direction. See Supabase's privacy policy for how they handle data they process for us.
  • Google Books API — when you search for a book to add, your search text (title/author you typed) is sent to Google's Books API to return results. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's privacy policy. We do not send your account details, existing shelf, or ratings to Google — only the search text you type, at the moment you search.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of MyBookLog, our users, or others.

Data Retention

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (see below), your bookshelf, ratings, and profile are permanently deleted immediately — this cannot be undone or recovered by us afterward.

Your Rights and Choices

Deleting your account (in-app)

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app: Menu → Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your bookshelf, ratings, profile, and login — this action is final and cannot be reversed.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't access your account, you can request deletion by emailing ravishu.arora@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. We'll confirm your identity and delete your account and all associated data.

Updating your information

You can update your first and last name at any time from Menu → Profile. Your email address currently cannot be changed in-app; contact us at ravishu.arora@gmail.com if you need it updated.

Children's Privacy

MyBookLog is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ravishu.arora@gmail.com. Upon receiving such a request, we will take reasonable steps to verify it (similar to how we verify any other account-deletion request — see "Requesting deletion without the app" above) before deleting the applicable information, to guard against false or malicious reports.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including relying on our backend provider's encryption in transit and at rest, and database access rules that restrict your data to only your own account. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
